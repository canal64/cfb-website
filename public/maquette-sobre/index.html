01 What is $CFB?

The meme that came
before everything else.

$CFB is the first-ever meme launched on Qubic. It was distributed fairly via airdrop to the entire community. It began as a pure memecoin and grew a real use case: it's the main token of QubicBay, Qubic's NFT marketplace, and it introduced the ecosystem's first NFT collection, Qbros.

02 QubicBay · Burn mechanics

Burned to build
Qubic's NFT culture.

QubicBay is Qubic's definitive NFT marketplace. To launch a collection, creators buy and burn $CFB to obtain minting slots. Every new collection reduces the supply — fully automated by the Qbay smart contract.
