    01 What is $CFB?

    The meme that came before everything else.

    02 QubicBay · Burn mechanics

    Burned to build Qubic's NFT culture.

    QubicBay is Qubic's definitive NFT marketplace, where $CFB became deflationary. To launch an NFT collection, creators must buy and burn $CFB to obtain minting slots. Every new collection reduces the supply, fully automated and managed exclusively by the Qbay smart contract.

    04 Getting started

    3 simple steps to join the $CFB ecosystem.

    Step 01

    Choose your wallet

    A wallet is necessary to use, trade, or hold $CFB. Use the official Qubic Wallet app or the Qubic Web Wallet.

    Get Qubic Wallet ↗
    Step 02

    Configure your wallet

    Follow Qubic's official documentation. Write down your 55-letter seed offline and never share it: it's the only way to recover your $CFB.

    ⚠️
    Security notice: wallet seeds

    If you created your seed on an iPhone or iPad, or in Safari on a Mac, before October 18, 2026, create a new one with an up-to-date Qubic wallet (version 4.26.4 or later) and move your funds to it. Seeds made on Windows, Android or Linux are not on the affected list.

    ✓ Qubic will never ask for your seed. Nobody from $CFB will either.
