01 What is $CFB?

The meme that came before everything else.

02 QubicBay · Burn mechanics

Burned to build Qubic's NFT culture.

QubicBay is Qubic's definitive NFT marketplace, where $CFB became deflationary. To launch an NFT collection, creators must buy and burn $CFB to obtain minting slots. Every new collection reduces the supply — fully automated and managed exclusively by the Qbay smart contract.
